# Primary-source citation index

Research date: **2026-10-14**. This is a deduplicated citation inventory of the four evidence notes below, **not an independently tested product benchmark**. The index pass checked local files and Markdown reference resolution; it did not independently re-audit every product claim or re-fetch every external URL. The notes distinguish documentation, vendor claims, inference, and unknowns.

**111 unique primary-source URLs**: 110 substantive cited pages and one Lio identification homepage. They are organized into **30 publisher/product/project groups**, representing **25 commercial vendor organizations** and **3 protocol/standards groups**, across **6 inventory categories**. Microsoft 365 and Microsoft GraphRAG have separate navigation groups; Salesforce and its Slack product also have separate groups. These counts describe coverage, not market size.

## Normalization and integrity

Only exact URL matches and recognized tracking-parameter variants are eligible for merging. This snapshot required no cross-note merges. Meaningful query parameters are retained, including Salesforce article IDs/language/type and ServiceNow `contentId`. No assumption is made that distinct paths or trailing-slash variants have identical content.

Local checks found **no missing note files, unresolved citation references, or broken local Markdown targets** in the four input notes. External access status remains the responsibility of the cited evidence notes; this inventory does not assert that every link remains publicly accessible or that every feature is generally available. The machine-readable [source index](source-index.json) records URLs, titles, note mappings, preserved original URLs, group counts, and input-file hashes.
