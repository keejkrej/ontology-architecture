# Enterprise AI ontology architecture research

Research conducted on **14 October 2026**, using primary sources. A representative comparison of **23 products**, plus retrieval, graph, agent-runtime, and interoperability infrastructure. This is public-source research, not hands-on product benchmarking.

Start with the [main report](research/enterprise-ai-ontology-architectures.md). It explains the architectural families, compares products, distinguishes RAG from ontology and workflow state, and proposes a design for organization understanding and human-agent collaboration at enterprise scale.

The [interactive architecture explorer](research/architecture-explorer.html) is a self-contained HTML file; open it in a browser to explore the architecture, market, and shared-work model.

## Evidence notes

- [Enterprise platforms](research/enterprise-platforms.md): Palantir, Glean, Microsoft, Salesforce, ServiceNow.
- [Vertical agents](research/vertical-agents.md): Harvey, Legora, Lio, Zip, Ironclad, Sierra, Decagon, Hebbia.
- [Collaboration and infrastructure](research/collaboration-infrastructure.md): Atlassian, Notion, Slack; GraphRAG, Neo4j, Stardog, LangGraph, MCP, A2A.
- [Data platforms and assistants](research/data-platforms-and-assistants.md): Snowflake, Databricks, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini Enterprise, Celonis, Workday.
- [Source index](research/sources.md): deduplicated links to the primary evidence.

The reports distinguish **documented behavior**, **vendor claims**, **architectural interpretation**, and **public unknowns**. Architecture recommendations and the cross-functional procurement walkthrough are proposed designs, not assertions about undisclosed vendor internals.
